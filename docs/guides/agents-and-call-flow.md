# Agents and the call flow

Your phone is answered by a team. **My assistant → Call flow** (with **Show all features** on) shows the team as cards: who answers each line, who a call can be passed to, and what each one may use.

![Call flow](../screenshots/app-call-flow.png)

## The team

- **The receptionist** answers every call, or the calls on one line if you link a line to it. It greets the caller, helps with most things, and passes on what someone else does better.
- **Specialists** are AI agents with one job each: bookings, sales, customer service, events and groups, or anything you write. Each has its own name, voice and instructions.
- **People** are real staff. When a caller asks for them (or a complaint needs a person), the assistant puts the caller on hold, rings that person's phone or paired companion app, briefs them, and leaves the call to them.

**Set up a team** adds a whole team for a kind of business, already linked. **Add AI agent** offers ready-made roles. **Add person** adds a member of staff with their number.

## Passing calls

Drag from a card's orange **+** dot onto another card to link them. A link means "may pass calls to". Tap a link to remove it. When a call is passed:

1. The caller hears hold music for a moment.
2. The next agent picks up **in their own voice**, already knowing what the caller needs (a short brief), so the caller never repeats themselves.
3. If the caller asks for someone by name ("Can I speak to Mia?"), they're put through, even when the name is misheard ("me a", "Mayor").

## What each agent may use

Tap a card to choose its:

- **Abilities:** take messages, book, take orders.
- **Systems:** the business apps and other connected services (MCP) it may use. A caller never reaches the owner's own tools or another business's data.
- **Skills and documents:** a menu PDF, your policies, a script. It answers from these.
- **Voice, greeting and personality.**

An agent given only what it needs is faster and makes fewer mistakes.

## Managing a big flow

- **Zoom:** use a trackpad pinch, the mouse wheel, or the −/+ buttons. Drag the background to move around.
- **Fit** shows everything. **Tidy up** sets the cards out neatly: the receptionist on the left, each step a call can take in the next column, people last.
- **Go to an agent** jumps to any card by name. **Taller canvas** gives you more room.
- Link a **phone line** to the agent that should answer it by dragging from the line's dot.

## Skills

A skill is a document with instructions for a kind of situation. For example, [`skills/restaurant-skill.md`](../../skills/restaurant-skill.md) covers allergies, complaints and large groups. Add them in **My assistant → Skills** and **Knowledge**. They're searched by meaning, so the right part is used when it's needed.
